Questions Brides Ask

Custom gowns,
honestly answered.

How much does it cost?

Fully custom gowns start at $800. The final quote depends on design, fabric and detail work, and you get an exact number at the free consultation, before any deposit.

Can you work from a photo?

Yes, that is our specialty. Send photos of the dress you love, ideally when you book, and we recreate the look to your measurements.

Do I need my measurements?

No. We measure you at the studio. Curious what gets measured? See the measurement guide for every step.

Can I see it before it is made?

Yes. Your free consultation includes a complimentary preview video of you wearing the gown, before a single stitch is sewn.

What if it needs adjusting?

When your gown arrives we schedule a final fitting, and any refinements are done in-house by a seamstress with 40+ years of alterations experience.

When should I start?

The earlier the better. Tell us your wedding date at the consultation and we will confirm a comfortable timeline for making, delivery and the final fitting.

The first consultation is free.
The preview video too.

Bring your photos, your venue and your vision. We will measure you, recommend styles, and show you a preview of yourself in the gown, all before you spend a dollar.
