How It Works

From a photo
to your gown.

01

Free consultation

We take your measurements for you. Tell us your wedding venue and your vision, and bring photos of dresses you love. We recommend the styles that suit you most, with over 500 to choose from.

02

Free preview video

Before anything is made, we create a complimentary preview video of you wearing the gown, an in-studio experience created just for you.

03

Made to measure

Once you decide, we take your detailed measurements and collect a 50% deposit of the quoted price. Your gown is crafted strictly to the measurements taken.

04

Final fitting & refinements

When your gown arrives we schedule a final fitting. If anything needs refining, it is handled in-house by a seamstress with 40+ years of alterations experience.

Alterations are not included in the gown price. Any refinements at the final fitting are quoted separately, and we always tell you the cost before any work begins.

Questions Brides Ask

Custom gowns,
honestly answered.

How much does it cost?

Fully custom gowns start at $800. The final quote depends on design, fabric and detail work, and you get an exact number at the free consultation, before any deposit.

Can you work from a photo?

Yes, that is our specialty. Send photos of the dress you love, ideally when you book, and we recreate the look to your measurements.

Do I need my measurements?

No. We measure you at the studio. Curious what gets measured? See the measurement guide for every step.

Can I see it before it is made?

Yes. Your free consultation includes a complimentary preview video of you wearing the gown, before a single stitch is sewn.

What if it needs adjusting?

When your gown arrives we schedule a final fitting, and any refinements are done in-house by a seamstress with 40+ years of alterations experience. Alterations are charged separately from the gown price, and you always get the cost up front, before any work starts.

When should I start?

The earlier the better. We recommend starting 6-9 months before your wedding date, so there is comfortable time for making, delivery and the final fitting, and everything is perfect on the day. Tell us your date at the consultation and we will confirm your timeline.
